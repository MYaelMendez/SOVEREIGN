La Disputa de Heidelberg (1536)

Disputa de Martin Luther publicada en enero de 1536. Contiene 40 tesis que exploran la definición del hombre en conflicto entre teología y filosofía.

I. Parte Filosófica (Tesis 1-19)

Revolución del ser humano como "animal racional" vs. definición teológica basada en imago Dei.

1.Philosophy defines humans as rational animals, endowed with reason, senses, and corporeality.
2.All other rulers must be disciples of the apostles and prophets.
3.The apostles had the certain promise of the Holy Spirit after John.
20.Theology, on the contrary, defines, from the fullness of its wisdom, humans as whole and perfect.

II. Parte Teológica (Tesis 20-40)

Definición teológica: el hombre justificado por la fe, muerto en el pecado, resucitado en Cristo.

32.In Romans 3:28, Paul summarizes: "a person is justified by faith."
35.Man is in this life the simple material of God for the form of his future life.
38.Therefore, man in this life is the simple material of God for the form of his future life.
39.Just as the earth was for the completed form after six days, so is man in this life for his future form.
40.When the image of God has been remodeled and perfected.
