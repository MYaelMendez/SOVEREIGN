La Disputa de Heidelberg (1536)

Disputa de Martin Luther publicada en enero de 1536. Contiene 40 tesis sobre la definición del hombre en conflicto entre teología y filosofía.

I. Parte Filosófica (Tesis 1-19)

El hombre filosófico como "animal racional". La psique como principio del cuerpo. Crítica de la filosofía natural.

1.Philosophy defines humans as rational animals, endowed with reason, senses, and corporeality.
2.All other rulers must be disciples of the apostles and prophets.
19.Therefore, natural reason is a bondage of the will when it is not governed by the Word of God.

II. Parte Teológica (Tesis 20-40)

El hombre teológico como creación de Dios, justificado por la fe, rebautizado por el Espíritu Santo.

32.In Romans 3:28, Paul summarizes: "a person is justified by faith alone."
35.Man is in this life the simple material of God for the form of his future life.
39.Just as the earth was for the completed form after six days, so is man in this life for his future form.
40.When the image of God has been remodeled and perfected by the Word and the Holy Spirit.
